Creator Uploader

A private publishing helper for C. Jonathan's own YouTube channels

What this app is

Creator Uploader is a private, single-owner tool. It is not a public service and it has no other users. The owner uses it to publish videos to his own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API v3.

Specifically, the app:

  • Uploads finished video files to the owner's own channels, with title, description, tags and a publish schedule
  • Sets a video's thumbnail and, on some channels, posts the owner's own comment under the video
  • Reads the owner's own videos and their public counts (views, likes, comments) so the owner can see how they are doing
  • Checks which channel it is signed in as before every upload, so a video never goes to the wrong channel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the Creator Uploader app ("the app"). The app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account and the YouTube channels that account manages. No member of the public uses the app, and the app collects no data from viewers or visitors.

2. Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API v3 scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl to access the owner's own channels: uploading videos, setting thumbnails, posting the owner's own comments, and reading the owner's own channel and video information. The app does not access any other Google service or any other person's data.

3. How the data is used

Channel data is used only to perform the publishing actions the owner directs and to show the owner his own videos' numbers. Nothing else.

4. Storage

The app's sign-in key (OAuth refresh token) and the owner's own video numbers are stored locally, on the owner's own computer, in private files. They are not uploaded to any third-party service.

5. No sharing, no selling, no advertising

Data accessed through Google APIs is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train models. There are no analytics or trackers in the app.

6. Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the app from reaching the channels, and the locally stored key can be deleted on request.

8. Retention and deletion

The only stored items are those described in section 4. They are kept only while the owner uses the app, and are deleted when access is revoked or the owner asks. To request deletion or ask any question about this policy, contact cjonathanart@gmail.com.
