It's Lying To You Uploader

The private helper app for the YouTube channel It's Lying To You (@ItsLyingToYou)

What this app is

It's Lying To You Uploader is a private, single-owner tool. It is not a public service and it has no other users. The channel owner uses it for one job: publishing the channel's own videos and organising its playlists through the official YouTube Data API v3.

Specifically, the app:

  • Uploads finished video files to the owner's own channel
  • Creates and manages the channel's playlists
  • Sets video titles, descriptions and privacy status at upload time

Google Cloud project: its-lying-to-you-uploader. Contact: cjonathanart@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the It's Lying To You Uploader app ("the app"). The app is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel It's Lying To You, on that owner's own account. No member of the public uses the app, and the app collects no data from viewers or visitors.

2. Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API v3 scopes youtube.upload and youtube to access the owner's own YouTube channel — uploading videos, reading the channel's own playlist and video lists, and creating playlists. The app does not access any other Google service or any other person's data.

3. How the data is used

Channel data is used only to perform the publishing actions the owner directs: uploading the owner's videos, setting their titles, descriptions and privacy status, and managing the owner's playlists. Nothing else.

4. Storage

The app's sign-in key (OAuth refresh token) is stored locally, on the owner's own machine, in a private file readable only by the owner's assistant software. It is not uploaded to any third-party service.

5. No sharing, no selling, no advertising

Data accessed through Google APIs is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train models. There are no analytics or trackers in the app.

6. Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the app from reaching the channel, and the locally stored key can be deleted on request.

8. Retention and deletion

The only stored item is the sign-in key described in section 4. It is kept only while the owner uses the app, and is deleted when access is revoked or the owner asks. To request deletion or ask any question about this policy, contact cjonathanart@gmail.com.
